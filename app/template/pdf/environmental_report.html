Methodology

Environmental impact avoidance is calculated based on extending the lifecycle of devices rather than requiring new device manufacturing. Each refurbished device is assumed to avoid the manufacture of one equivalent new device.

Manufacturing ("embedded") impact figures are sourced from the Boavizta API, which applies Negaoctet's methodology using the ADEME Base Impacts database. Impacts are based on generic device archetypes, not specific models. All values represent impacts avoided thanks to extended lifespan.

Calculation method: total impact avoided = number of devices × impact per device (manufacturing phase). Use-phase electricity and end-of-life are excluded because a refurbished device has the same in-use footprint as a new device. Values are point estimates and are not third-party verified.

Methodology version: {{ report.methodology_version }}.

Additional environmental benefits

  • Waste reduction: devices diverted from electronic waste streams, extending product lifecycles.
  • Resource conservation: reduced demand for new raw materials, including rare-earth metals.
  • Circular economy: extended product lifecycle supporting sustainable consumption patterns.
  • Social enterprise: supporting a local non-profit reducing e-waste, improving digital inclusion and employing people facing barriers to work.

Data sources and assumptions

  • Manufacturing impact data sourced from the Boavizta API (api.boavizta.org).
  • Impact assessment methodology: Negaoctet's methodology via the ADEME Base Impacts database.
  • Generic device archetype data used for manufacturing-phase impacts.
  • Assumption: one refurbished device replaces one new device purchase.
  • Assessment focuses on manufacturing-phase impacts avoided through lifecycle extension.
  • Assessment excludes operational emissions from the refurbishment process, which are negligible.
